listener-marks.woff
-------------------
A subset of DejaVu Sans Mono containing ONLY the 17 characters that carry the
listener log's notation — the four tone letters, the cups, the bits, the tally,
the slots, the stems, and the middot. Built 2026-08-01 with fontTools from
DejaVuSansMono.ttf.

WHY IT EXISTS. Inconsolata, the page face, contains none of these characters, so
every mark in the book was being drawn by whatever fallback font the reader's
device happened to pick — a different shape on a phone than on a desk, different
advance widths (which is why "monospace" columns did not line up), and tofu on any
device lacking coverage. The marks are a deliberate, closed set declared in
plans/MARK_INVENTORY.md; they should not be at the mercy of a fallback chain.

REBUILD. The charset is exactly the inventory. Scan the rendered DOM for the mark
spans, then:
  python3 -m fontTools.subset DejaVuSansMono.ttf --unicodes=<list> \
      --flavor=woff --output-file=fonts/listener-marks.woff
