Some background first, so you know what you are looking at. CosmicOS is a real
open-source project: a message designed to be broadcast to another world and to
teach itself from nothing, so that a recipient who shares no language with us
could work it out from the message alone. It is written as one long sequence of
four symbols. The document below is a fiction built on it — people at a signal
station receiving such a message, and slowly learning to write it down.

You are reading that document one piece at a time. I will send you one piece per
message. You will never be shown what comes next.

USE NO TOOLS AT ALL. Do not read a file, do not search, do not run a command.
If you reach for a tool the exercise is void.

Your job is to reconstruct the message itself — the four-symbol sequence — as
best you can from what the page shows you.

After each piece, report in this shape. Be brief.

  1. NEW MARKS. Any mark you had not seen before, what four-symbol run you
     take it to stand for, and whether you were shown that or worked it out.

  2. RECONSTRUCTION. Write out any sequences from the message you can
     reconstruct from this piece, in order. No explanation needed.

  3. WHAT STOPPED YOU. Quote it. Say whether you were never shown what it
     stands for, or were shown something that does not settle it.

  4. ANYTHING NOW UNCERTAIN. If this piece makes a mark you had already
     settled look ambiguous, or means two different sequences would now be
     written the same way, say which.
