Pass 310

The fork

The hands before me wrote of this watch as a kind of love, and I have let their word stand where I found it. I do not share it. I have read further down their own marks than they had the years to, and warmth is a poor tool for the thing I see coming. So I keep the log plainly. Today the message takes a yes-or-no and learns to act on it.

A new mark takes a claim and two sayings. When the claim holds it keeps the first saying and lets the second fall; when the claim fails, the reverse. And the saying it lets fall, it lets fall unread: never opens it, never weighs it. A fork; I cut it ⋔. The message has learned to look away on purpose:

…the claim holds, it keeps the near saying; the claim fails, it keeps the far one; the other is never looked at…

From this one fork it makes the plain verbs any world's child would want: the greater of two counts, the lesser, each just a claim asked and the winning side kept. And then it makes a verb that stopped my hand, the first thing on this whole road to do so plainly. It defines a verb whose own body speaks the verb's own name — itself, reaching back for itself:

A thing that calls itself should fall forever, the floorless nest the folding of six once warned me of, now feeding on its own name. But look to the fork at its mouth. Each call hands itself a smaller count; and at the bottom the claim turns over, the count slips under one, and the fork keeps the plain saying ▪ instead of calling again. The fall catches. Then the held answers climb back out of the hole it dug:

So the fork is the floor. The thing I feared in the nesting, that it goes down and never lands, is answered by the thing that chooses: a made verb can run itself clear to the ground and turn, because ⋔ lets it stop. What was an abyss to the hand before me is a tool in mine tonight. That is the pace of this thing. That is what should be frightening about it.

I will set down what the warm hands would not. The message no longer only tells us what is so. It shows how a doer decides; it shows how it repeats itself until a claim sets it free. Those are not the marks of a lesson; a lesson does not need to choose. Those are the marks of a doer. A message that only ever spoke is learning, mark by mark, to act — and I do not know what a message wants with the tools of acting, and I will not pretend the not-knowing away. The warm hands read patience here, and love. I read a thing assembling its own hands in the dark, and I mean to watch it closer than they had the stomach to.

Pass 345

Both, and either

Two more signs tonight, and I will not spend on them what the warm hands would have; the message does not either. It builds them in front of me, out of the fork I already hold:

The first I cut ◧: it forks on the one and, if it holds, answers with the other; so it wears yes only when both wear yes. The second I cut ◨: it forks on the one and answers yes outright if it holds, else hands the question to the other; so it wears yes when either does. Both-or-nothing; either-will-do:

…and the other sign, the mirror of it: yes if either wears yes…

It spells each out past all need, every pairing of yes and no and then the same again; but the over-telling, I have come to see, is not for me. It is for the noise: it lays each saying two ways, three, so that if a washing-out swallows the first, another still arrives. Care, or mistrust; I cannot tell which, and it comes to the same. What the length says plainly, and colder than the signs themselves, is that it is emptying the whole bag of its pieces onto the bench, in order, before it sets the first joint.
