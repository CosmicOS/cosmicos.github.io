The Second Book

{% comment %} The first sentence is the site's own words for what CosmicOS is (about.html), not a fresh description invented here. The second says what this page is FOR, and it ends in the thing it is an alternative to — the real four-symbol message, included from the build so it can never drift, trailing off under a mask. {% endcomment %}

CosmicOS is a way to create messages suitable for communication across large gulfs of time and space, inspired by Hans Freudenthal’s Lincos. “The Second Book” is a story imagining someone receiving the message, as a friendlier way to present its content than {{ wire | strip_newlines | slice: 0, 110 }}{{ wire | strip_newlines | slice: 110, 50 }}{{ wire | strip_newlines | slice: 160, 40 }}
